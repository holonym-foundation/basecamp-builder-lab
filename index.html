Exercise
  1. Press Start. Watch three samples arrive and note the simulated range.
  2. Change Range from 200 to 400. Press Reset, then Start.
  3. Compare the new SIMULATED RANGE line with the first one. It is roughly twice as wide.
  4. Bonus: set the threshold to 5 and watch for a would rebalance line when the pool moves.

The range snaps to the pool's tick spacing, so ±200 ticks becomes a 360-tick band on a 60-spacing pool. Prices move in ticks: one tick is a 0.01% price step.
